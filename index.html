MedicoApp Commercial release in preparation

Windows desktop software for medical practices

Medical practice software, built to stay close to the practice.

MedicoApp is a local-first desktop application designed for francophone medical practices. It brings patient administration, consultations, medical documents and day-to-day practice workflows together in a clear Windows experience.

Contact MedicoApp Support
Local-first Designed around local operation on the doctor's Windows computer, without cloud patient records.
Practice-focused Built around the everyday documentation and administrative workflows of a medical practice.
Privacy-conscious The public website does not collect patient information and is separate from the MedicoApp clinical environment.
